Yang is a PhD student in the School of Biomedical Engineering & Imaging Sciences at King's College London, supervised by Dr Alejandro Granados, Dr Nicholas Raison, Prof Toktam Mahmoodi and Prof Prokar Dasgupta. His research focuses on federated learning and distributed optimization for surgical data science, with the overarching goal of building the Internet of Surgical Things (IoST).

Yang holds an MSc in Integrated Machine Learning Systems from UCL (Distinction with Top Project Prize), where his master's project investigated the security of consumer IoT devices, and a first-class BEng in Mechatronics, Robotics and Automation Engineering from the University of Birmingham. Alongside his PhD, he works part-time as a software engineer at kioko.
